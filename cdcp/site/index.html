Common questions

Who is eligible for the Canadian Dental Care Plan?

You qualify if all four are true: you're a Canadian resident for tax purposes, you filed last year's tax return, your adjusted family net income is under $90,000, and you don't have access to private dental insurance.

Can I get CDCP if I have insurance through work?

Generally no. Having access to private/employer/pension/group dental insurance disqualifies you — even if you decline it. Coverage through a government social program does not count, so you may still qualify in that case.

Does CDCP cover 100% of my dental bill?

If your income is under $70,000, CDCP pays 100% of eligible costs. But it pays against established fees that can be lower than what a dentist charges, so you may still owe a difference. Ask your dentist if they bill at the CDCP rate.

How do I apply?

Apply through Service Canada online or by phone; the CRA validates your income. Once approved, Sun Life sends a welcome package and your coverage start date. Official application ↗

What if I don't qualify?

You still have options — private dental insurance, dental discount/membership plans, financing, or provincial programs — and we can connect you with a dentist regardless. Run the eligibility check and we'll point you the right way.
